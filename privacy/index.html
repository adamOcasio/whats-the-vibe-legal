what's the vibe? privacy policy

Effective October 17, 2026

what's the vibe? ("the app", "we") is an app for finding bars and clubs in New York City through recent reports about their vibe, which never show who chose which vibe. It is run by Adam Ocasio. This policy explains what the app collects, why, how long it is kept, and how to delete it.

In short: you can browse without an account. An account holds your email address. There are no public profiles, photos or follower lists, and other people never see which vibes you chose, except on your night card. Night cards are optional, and you have none unless you make one. While yours is on, people who shared a vibe at the same venue as you that night, with their own card on, see your first name, your Instagram username and the vibes you dropped there that night (never "Let's mingle") from the next 4 a.m. until the 4 a.m. after that, and so know you were there. The app uses your location only while it is open, and never stores your exact position. You can delete your account and its data in the app, or by writing to us.

What we collect

When you browse without an account. Nothing that identifies you. To list nearby venues, the app sends the area you are looking at: either a neighborhood you picked or your phone's position rounded to about 250 meters. Each run of the app also sends a random ID, made fresh every time the app starts and never stored on your phone, so the service can limit how many requests one phone makes. We keep that ID with request counters for a few hours at most. We do not store the area or position.

When you sign in. You sign in with Apple, Google or, where offered, an emailed link. Our account system keeps:

  • your email address (with Apple's Hide My Email, a private relay address Apple gives us instead);
  • which way you signed in and that service's account identifier;
  • the basic profile details that service sends at sign-in (for Google, your name and the link to your profile picture; Apple sends only your email). The app does not show or use them;
  • your sign-in sessions: when each started and was last used, and the IP address and device or app version it came from, which our sign-in system records to keep sessions secure.

We use your account to let you share vibes, keep your saved places in sync, send feedback and, if you choose, make a night card, and to limit abuse (for example, one current report per person per venue).

When you share a vibe. You choose a venue and one or two vibes (and optionally a music genre). To check that you are at the venue, the app reads your phone's location while the app is open and sends it with its accuracy and time. Our server checks that you are within 150 meters of the venue and then discards the position; it is never stored. The vibe itself is stored with your account for 90 minutes so that you have one current report per venue, then deleted (within an hour after it expires). If your night card is on, we also record that you were at that venue that night (see Night cards below).

Apart from night cards, other people see only totals for a venue: how many recent reports it has and which vibes they chose. "Let's mingle" appears only once at least three people chose it. Nobody sees who reported what.

Night cards. A night card is optional: you have none unless you make one in Account → Your card, and you can turn it off at any time. Share my card after 4 a.m. starts on for a new card; turn it off before you tap Save card if you don't want it shared yet. The first time you save a card with it on, the app asks you to agree to our Terms of use, which allow no objectionable content or abusive users. We keep your card with your account. It holds:

  • the first name you type (up to 30 characters) and the Instagram username you type;
  • whether your card is on (Share my card after 4 a.m.);
  • a random card ID, which stays the same until you delete your card; other people's apps receive it with your card and use it only to block or report it. Because it does not change, someone who sees your card on different nights could tell it is the same card even after you change your name or username; a card you delete and make again gets a new ID;
  • when you last saved it.

A night runs from 4 a.m. to 4 a.m. New York time. While your card is on, each vibe you share records your account at that venue for that night: the account, the venue, the night's date, the vibes of your latest vibe there (never "Let's mingle"; one of only "Let's mingle" changes nothing) and whether it was a test vibe, which only accounts we set up for testing can send (see below). The record holds no time of day and no position. Turning your card on also records the vibes you shared tonight that are still current.

From the 4 a.m. that ends the night until the next 4 a.m., everyone recorded at the same venue that night sees the others' cards, as long as their own card is still on and neither has blocked the other. Cards are a swap: while your card is off, nobody sees it and you see nobody's. People who see your card learn your first name, your Instagram username, the vibes you dropped there that night (your latest, never "Let's mingle") and, from the venue it is listed under, that you shared a vibe there that night. They never see what time you were there, where you are now, whether you chose "Let's mingle", your email or anything else from your account. Tapping a card opens the Instagram profile for the username on it. Real cards never show before 4 a.m. Test cards come only from accounts we set up for testing, show only to such accounts, and can be revealed early. Accounts we set up for testing include test accounts a tester makes for itself in the app: each holds only a first name from a fixed list (such as Maya) and an email address made from the tester's own, which is never sent anything. They send test vibes only, and deleting the tester's account deletes them too.

We delete night records within an hour after that night's cards stop showing, so they last at most about two days. Turning your card off or deleting it deletes them at once, for every night.

Every card from another person has Report and Block; sample cards, which belong to no one, have neither. Blocking hides your two cards from each other for good, and the other person is not told. We keep the block, which names both accounts and when it was made, until either account is deleted. Reporting asks for a reason (not their Instagram, offensive, harassment or something else) and blocks too. A report keeps your account, the reported account, the reason, the first name and username on the card when you sent it, when you sent it, and the outcome of our review and when we made it. We keep reports until either account is deleted.

We review every report. When a report shows that a card breaks our Terms of use, we delete the card and ban the account within 24 hours of the report; we may also do so without a report. A banned account's card stops showing at once, but the account and its card, blocks, reports, saved places and feedback are kept until the account is deleted. While an account is banned it cannot use Delete account in the app; write to us at the address below and we will delete it.

We refuse first names and usernames that use slurs, sexual terms or adult promotion. We do not check that an Instagram username belongs to the person who typed it, so use only your own. People who see a card can report it, and anyone, including the owner of an Instagram username used on someone else's card, can write to us at the address below and we will remove that card. What people find on your Instagram profile depends on your Instagram settings.

To stop sharing, open Your card, turn off Share my card after 4 a.m. and tap Save card: your card leaves every list at once and your night records are deleted. The card itself stays saved, unseen, until you tap Delete my card, which deletes it. Blocks and reports stay until your account or the other account is deleted.

Venue history. Every 15 minutes we record each venue's public totals, only where at least three people reported, to show its last 7 nights. These records count reports; they contain no account and cannot be traced to anyone. We keep them for 8 days.

Saved places. Places you save are stored on your phone. When you are signed in, they are also stored with your account (as venue IDs only, at most 500) so they sync between your phones.

Feedback. If you suggest a missing place or report one closed, we keep your note, the venue or place name, and, for a suggestion, the center of the area you were looking at (rounded to about 100 meters), with your account. We read it to review it and keep it, with the outcome of our review, until you delete your account.

On your phone. The app keeps your sign-in session, your saved places, a note that your account agreed to the Terms of use (your account's ID and the date of the terms you agreed to; we never receive it) and a few app settings on your phone. They are removed when you sign out (your session), delete your account (its saved places and that note) or delete the app. The app stores no night card, yours or anyone else's, on your phone.

What we do not collect. No contacts, photos, microphone, advertising identifier, tracking across other apps or websites, precise location in the background, or analytics about how you use the app. The app does not connect to your Instagram account or read anything from it; a card holds only the username you type. We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not show ads based on it. No third party tracks you across apps or websites through the app either, so the app works the same whether or not your device or browser sends a Do Not Track signal.

Location permission

The app asks for location "while using the app" only. It uses it to list venues near you and, when you share a vibe, to check that you are at the venue. If you decline, you can still browse by picking a neighborhood; sharing a vibe needs your location. The app never reads your location in the background.

Who processes your information

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and server functions in the United States.
  • Apple and Google handle Sign in with Apple and Sign in with Google under their own privacy policies; they tell us only what is described above.
  • Google Maps opens when you tap View on Google Maps or Directions. That happens in Google Maps or your browser, under Google's privacy policy; the app sends Google nothing itself.
  • Instagram (Meta) opens when you tap someone's night card. That happens in Instagram or your browser, under Meta's privacy policy; the app sends Instagram nothing itself.

Venue names, addresses and hours come from open data (Overture Maps and OpenStreetMap). They are about places, not people.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of people using the app.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Account and tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything tied to it: your current vibes (they leave venue totals at once), your night card (it leaves every list at once) and night records, the blocks and card reports you made or that name you, the saved places synced to it, any feedback you sent, and your sign-in sessions. It cannot be undone. Venue history totals, which contain no account, stay until they expire.

While your account is banned, it cannot use Delete account; write to us at the address below and we will delete it.

If you signed in with Apple, the app asks Apple to confirm and then disconnects the app from your Apple ID. If that step cannot finish, the app tells you how to do it yourself: in your iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Sign-In & Security, then Sign in with Apple, choose what's the vibe? and stop using your Apple ID with it.

You can also ask us to delete your account, or to tell you what we hold about you or correct it, by writing to the address below. Depending on where you live (for example, California or the European Union), you may have further rights, such as to access or port your information or to object to how it is used; contact us and we will respond within the time the law requires.

Children

The app is about bars and clubs and is not meant for anyone under 21. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Information travels encrypted (HTTPS). Accounts, vibes, night cards, blocks, card reports and feedback are stored where only our server functions can read them, apart from us reading card reports and feedback to review them; your synced saved places can be read only by you and those functions. Other people can see venue totals and, only if you make one and leave it on, your night card.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update it here with a new effective date, and tell you in the app when the change is significant.
