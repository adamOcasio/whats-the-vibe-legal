what's the vibe? terms of use

Effective October 17, 2026

These terms are an agreement between you and Adam Ocasio, who runs what's the vibe? ("the app", "we"), an app for finding bars and clubs in New York City through recent reports about their vibe. By signing in, or by agreeing when the app asks before you first share your night card, you accept them. If you don't accept them, don't sign in; you can still browse without an account. The privacy policy explains what the app collects and how to delete it.

In short: there is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Use only your own first name and Instagram username on your card, be kind, and report or block anyone who isn't. When a report shows that a card breaks these terms, we remove the card and ban the account that made it within 24 hours.

Who can use the app

You must be 21 or older to use the app. Use your own account, keep your sign-in to yourself, and don't make another account to get around a block or a ban.

No tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. This covers everything you put into the app: your night card's first name and Instagram username, the vibes you share, reports about cards, and feedback you send. Don't use the app to:

  • post slurs or hateful content, or demean people for their race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age or anything like it;
  • harass, bully, threaten, stalk or intimidate anyone, or encourage anyone else to;
  • post sexual content, or promote adult services, escorts or other adult content;
  • pretend to be someone else, or put an Instagram username that isn't yours on your card;
  • promote violence, illegal drugs or anything else illegal;
  • spam, advertise, run scams or ask people for money;
  • share someone else's private information;
  • share vibes from a place you aren't at, or report cards in bad faith;
  • get around a block, a ban, the app's word filter, its location check or its limits, or interfere with the app, copy its data in bulk or use it in a way it isn't meant to be used.

We may decide that other content or behavior is objectionable or abusive too.

What happens when someone breaks these terms

We review every report. When a report shows that a card breaks these terms, we act within 24 hours of the report: we delete the card, so it leaves every list at once, and ban the account that made it. We may also remove content or ban an account without a report, and without warning, when we find that it breaks these terms.

A banned account can no longer share vibes or a card, and its card stops showing at once. While an account is banned, its owner can't delete it in the app; write to us and we will delete it. Making a new account after a ban breaks these terms too.

Report, block and contact us

Every card from another person in Account → Last night's cards has a menu with Report and Block:

  • Report asks why (Not their Instagram, Offensive name or username, Harassment or Something else), sends the card to us for review, and blocks it too.
  • Block hides your two cards from each other for good. The other person isn't told.

You can also reach us any time with Account → Contact us, or by email at the address below: for example about a card that uses your Instagram username, about someone's behavior, or about these terms. We read every message.

Night cards

Your night card is optional, and you have none unless you make one in Account → Your card. While it is on, people who shared a vibe at the same spot as you that night, with their own card on, see your first name, your Instagram username and the vibes you dropped there that night, from the next 4 a.m. until the 4 a.m. after that. Put only your own first name and your own Instagram username on it. Turn it off or delete it at any time.

We don't check who anyone is. A card shows only what its owner typed, and what you find on someone's Instagram profile is up to them and Instagram. If you meet people through the app, take the care you would with anyone you meet on a night out.

Vibes and venue information

A vibe is a recent report from someone who said they were there; counts are reports, not people inside. Share vibes only where you are. Venue names, addresses, hours and vibes can be wrong or out of date, so check before you go, and enjoy the night responsibly.

Your content

What you put on your card stays yours. You let us store it and show it to the people the app shows it to, as the privacy policy describes, for as long as it stays in the app. You are responsible for what you post.

The app as it is

The app is free and offered as it is, without promises that it will always work, be available or be accurate. As far as the law allows, we aren't responsible for losses that come from using it, from information in it, or from what other people post or do, in the app or when you meet them.

Ending your use

You can stop using the app at any time, and delete your account in Account → Delete account. We may suspend or end any account that breaks these terms.

Changes

If we change these terms, we will post the new version here with a new effective date. The app asks you to agree to it before you next share your card.
