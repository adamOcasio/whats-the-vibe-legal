what's the vibe? privacy policy

Effective October 15, 2026

what's the vibe? ("the app", "we") is an app for finding bars and clubs in New York City through recent, anonymous reports about their vibe. It is run by Adam Ocasio. This policy explains what the app collects, why, how long it is kept, and how to delete it.

In short: you can browse without an account. An account holds your email address and nothing public: there are no profiles, names, photos or follower lists, and your reports are never shown with anything that identifies you. The app uses your location only while it is open, and never stores where you are. You can delete your account and its data in the app at any time.

What we collect

When you browse without an account. Nothing that identifies you. To list nearby venues, the app sends the area you are looking at: either a neighborhood you picked or your phone's position rounded to about 250 meters. Each run of the app also sends a random ID, made fresh every time the app starts and never stored on your phone, so the service can limit how many requests one phone makes. We keep that ID with request counters for a few hours at most. We do not store the area or position.

When you sign in. You sign in with Apple, Google or, where offered, an emailed link. Our account system keeps:

  • your email address (with Apple's Hide My Email, a private relay address Apple gives us instead);
  • which way you signed in and that service's account identifier;
  • the basic profile details that service sends at sign-in (for Google, your name and the link to your profile picture; Apple sends only your email). The app does not show or use them;
  • your sign-in sessions: when each started and was last used, and the IP address and device or app version it came from, which our sign-in system records to keep sessions secure.

We use your account to let you share vibes, keep your saved places in sync and send feedback, and to limit abuse (for example, one current report per person per venue).

When you share a vibe. You choose a venue and one or two vibes (and optionally a music genre). To check that you are at the venue, the app reads your phone's location while the app is open and sends it with its accuracy and time. Our server checks that you are within 150 meters of the venue and then discards the position; it is never stored. The vibe itself is stored with your account for 90 minutes so that you have one current report per venue, then deleted (within an hour after it expires).

Other people see only totals for a venue: how many recent reports it has and which vibes they chose. "Let's mingle" appears only once at least three people chose it. Nobody sees who reported what.

Venue history. Every 15 minutes we record each venue's public totals, only where at least three people reported, to show its last 7 nights. These records count reports; they contain no account and cannot be traced to anyone. We keep them for 8 days.

Saved places. Places you save are stored on your phone. When you are signed in, they are also stored with your account (as venue IDs only, at most 500) so they sync between your phones.

Feedback. If you suggest a missing place or report one closed, we keep your note, the venue or place name, and, for a suggestion, the center of the area you were looking at (rounded to about 100 meters), with your account, until we review it.

On your phone. The app keeps your sign-in session, your saved places and a few app settings on your phone. They are removed when you sign out (your session), delete your account (its saved places) or delete the app.

What we do not collect. No contacts, photos, microphone, advertising identifier, tracking across other apps or websites, precise location in the background, or analytics about how you use the app. We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not show ads based on it.

Location permission

The app asks for location "while using the app" only. It uses it to list venues near you and, when you share a vibe, to check that you are at the venue. If you decline, you can still browse by picking a neighborhood; sharing a vibe needs your location. The app never reads your location in the background.

Who processes your information

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and server functions in the United States.
  • Apple and Google handle Sign in with Apple and Sign in with Google under their own privacy policies; they tell us only what is described above.
  • Google Maps opens when you tap View on Google Maps or Directions. That happens in Google Maps or your browser, under Google's privacy policy; the app sends Google nothing itself.

Venue names, addresses and hours come from open data (Overture Maps and OpenStreetMap). They are about places, not people.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of people using the app.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Account and tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything tied to it: your current vibes (they leave venue totals at once), the saved places synced to it, any feedback you sent, and your sign-in sessions. It cannot be undone. Venue history totals, which contain no account, stay until they expire.

If you signed in with Apple, the app asks Apple to confirm and then disconnects the app from your Apple ID. If that step cannot finish, the app tells you how to do it yourself: in your iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Sign-In & Security, then Sign in with Apple, choose what's the vibe? and stop using your Apple ID with it.

You can also ask us to delete your account, or to tell you what we hold about you or correct it, by writing to the address below. Depending on where you live (for example, California or the European Union), you may have further rights, such as to access or port your information or to object to how it is used; contact us and we will respond within the time the law requires.

Children

The app is about bars and clubs and is not meant for anyone under 21. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Information travels encrypted (HTTPS). Accounts, vibes and feedback are stored where only our server functions can read them, and your synced saved places only by you and those functions. Other people can see only venue totals.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update it here with a new effective date, and tell you in the app when the change is significant.
