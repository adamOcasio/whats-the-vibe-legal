what's the vibe?

what's the vibe? is an iPhone app for nights out in New York City. It lists bars and clubs in Brooklyn and Queens with recent reports of their vibe (Dancing, Chatty, Chill, Let’s mingle and Live DJ) from people who were there. The numbers count recent reports, not people inside.

Browsing needs no account. Sign in with Apple or Google to share a vibe from a venue you’re at, keep your saved places in sync and, if you choose, share a night card (a first name and an Instagram username) with others who were at the same venue that night and share theirs. Signing in with Google asks only for your email address and basic profile. The privacy policy explains what the app keeps and how to delete your account.
